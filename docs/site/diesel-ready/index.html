Diesel Ready

Diesel Ready

An unofficial study guide to looking after a small marine diesel engine: short lessons, quizzes, engine checklists and a fault finder for leisure boaters. Free, open source and private.

Coming soon on theApp Store Coming later onGoogle Play

Learn how your engine works

Eight modules and 35 short lessons in plain English: how a diesel works, fuel and bleeding, cooling, lubrication, electrics and starting, belts and stern gear, routine maintenance and fault finding, with simple diagrams.

Test yourself

Check your knowledge after each lesson, take a quiz on each module, a quick 10-question quiz or a 30-question mock test, and review the questions you missed. Every answer comes with an explanation.

Engine checklists

Pre-start checks with the engine off, built on the widely used WOBBLE memory aid (water, oil, belts, bilges, leaks and levels), then post-start checks with it running: water from the exhaust, warning lights, temperature, noise and smoke. Tick items off as you go and reset for next time. Ticks are never saved or sent anywhere, and every check links to the lesson that explains it.

Fault finder

Stop cards come first: what to do at once for low oil pressure, overheating, water coming in, suspected water in the engine, fuel spray, smoke or fire, and a leaking shaft seal. Then pick a symptom, such as overheating, won’t start or low oil pressure, to see the possible causes, what to check first and the lessons that explain more.

Works offline

Everything is built into the app, so it works in harbour or at sea with no signal.

Private by design

No account, no analytics, no adverts, no tracking. Your progress stays on your phone.

Open source
