Diesel Ready

Privacy policy

Last updated 19 November 2026

In short: Diesel Ready does not collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and the app works entirely offline.

Who we are

Diesel Ready is published by Meltemi Apps, an independent developer based in the United Kingdom. You can contact us at support@meltemiapps.co.uk.

Information we collect

None. Diesel Ready does not ask for your name, email address or any other personal information, and we have no servers that receive data from the app. No personal data is collected, stored off your device or transmitted.

Information kept on your device

Your lesson progress, quiz results and settings are saved on your device only. This information never leaves your device. You can delete it with Reset progress in the app’s About tab, and it is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Network

The app makes no network connections. It does not send any information about you over the internet. Links in the app, such as this page or the project on GitHub, open in your web browser or mail app, and include no information about you.

Analytics, advertising and tracking

Diesel Ready contains no analytics, crash reporting, advertising or tracking software, and does not track you across other apps or websites.

Children

Diesel Ready collects no personal data from anyone, including children under 13.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have rights to access, correct and delete personal data held about you. Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. If you have any question about your data protection rights under UK or EU law, contact us and we will reply within 30 days. You can also contact the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.

Unofficial and unaffiliated

Diesel Ready is an independent revision aid. It is not an official course and does not award any certificate. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to any training organisation, sailing association or engine manufacturer. The lessons and questions are original material written for the app. Any third-party names belong to their respective owners and are used only to describe subject matter.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above. Material changes will also be noted in the app’s release notes.
