Frequently asked questions

Is Diesel Ready an official course?

No. It is an independent revision aid. It is not an official course, does not award any certificate, and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to any training organisation, sailing association or engine manufacturer.

Does it replace my engine handbook?

No. The lessons give general guidance that applies to typical small marine diesels. Engines differ, so always follow your engine manufacturer’s handbook for procedures, settings and service intervals, and ask a qualified marine engineer if you are unsure.

Who wrote the content?

All lessons and questions are original, written for the app in plain English. They have been reviewed and approved by a qualified marine engineer. The content is shared under the CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 licence.

Does it work without a signal?

Yes. Everything is built into the app, and it never needs an internet connection.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-in, and your progress stays on your phone.

How do I start again?

Open the About tab and choose Reset progress. This clears your lesson progress and quiz results from your phone.

Is there an Android version?

Not yet. An Android version is planned after the iOS release.

I think an answer is wrong. How do I tell you?

Email us with the question and why you think it is wrong, or open an issue on GitHub Issues. Corrections are very welcome.

How do I report a bug?

Email us with your phone model, the app version (shown in the About tab) and what happened, or open an issue on GitHub Issues.
